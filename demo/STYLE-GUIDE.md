# EverGuard style guide

For anyone building an EverGuard surface: the deck, the agreement, the demo, or
anything Keenan builds next. One rail, so two or three different tools produce
work that looks like one brand.

## Name

**EVERGUARD**, one word. In the wordmark, EVER is bone and GUARD is orange. No
other name is ever used for this brand in any file, comment, or asset name.

## Color

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Signal orange, the accent and the vehicle | `#FF6A00` |
| Deep orange, hovers and gradients | `#CC4E00` |
| Black, page ground | `#1A1A1A` |
| Charcoal, cards and panels | `#2E2E2E` |
| Bone, body text | `#E6E6E6` |

Orange is never a background wash. It is the truck, the rule, the one live
element on a dark screen. If a layout has more than roughly one tenth orange,
it is wrong.

## Type

- Headers: **Orbitron**, 700 or 800, uppercase, letter spacing .04em to .08em.
- Body: **Montserrat**, 400 and 600, sentence case, line height about 1.6.
- No third family. No italic display type. Numbers in Orbitron.

## Motion

- Fast in, slow settle. Ease out, roughly 220ms to 420ms.
- Nothing bounces, nothing springs, nothing spins for decoration.
- The vehicle pass is the only big move. Everything else is small.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces movement with cuts and keeps every control.

## Imagery

- A cinematic black operations room. Camera HUD chrome: corner brackets, a REC
  dot, coordinates, scanlines, grain.
- One real photograph per surface at most, and it should be a camera, an
  operations space, or a vehicle.
- **Never:** sunset heroes, Diamond Head at golden hour, palm trees as framing,
  a person holding a coffee cup, twelve-icon feature grids, generic shield
  clip art.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Name the situation before naming the service.
- Say what it is, not what it unlocks. "Keenan builds the systems. On the
  island." beats "empowering your business through technology."
- Never "helps determine," never "solutions provider," never "brighter
  tomorrows" as a claim.
- The two seats, as written: Keenan builds the systems. Joseph is the CXO. You
  work in the business, he works on it. One team, not two vendors.

## Rules that are not style

- Everguard surfaces are private until Joey says otherwise. Access code at the
  edge, `noindex`, nothing linked publicly.
- Never commit the access code, the signed agreement, payment details, or
  personal contact information. This repository is public.
