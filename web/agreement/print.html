Partnership Agreement

Prepared for Chris Calderon
Initial partnership period: October 1 to December 31, 2026. An earlier start in September is available, prorated, by mutual agreement.

1. What this is

The work in front of Chris Calderon is broader than day-to-day IT: running current operations, separating technology from the former parent organization, standing up new offices and centers, building the digital side of the business, and supporting technology for her own security clients.

This document sets out who does what, how decisions are made, what happens in the first ninety days, and the commercial terms. It is a proposal and a statement of scope. It is not itself the final service agreement.

2. The two seats

Keenan Miller Technology Solutions — technology, execution, local presence

Keenan builds and operates the systems, on the island: stack, cameras, access, migrations, and the days that need a body on site. Keenan decides how something is built, which tools and vendors do it, and the sequence of the technical work.

Joe CXO — executive, operational and financial strategy

Joseph serves as the CXO. Chris works in the business; Joseph works on it, including what gets fixed first so the same problem is not discussed four times. Joseph decides nothing on his own: he recommends priority, structure and spend, and Chris decides.

Chris Calderon — owner, final say

Chris remains the owner and approves budget, hiring, anything touching a client contract, and anything either party flags for her decision. The intent is that fewer matters require her attention, not that authority moves away from her.

The engagement is delivered as one team rather than two separate vendors.

4. Working rhythm and response

  • A 30-minute working call each week, on a fixed day and time.
  • A written summary at the end of each month: what shipped, what is next, what is blocked and on what.
  • A working review at the end of the initial period.

Response commitments. Systems down, meaning cameras, dispatch, access control or telephony are out: response the same business day, with a call back within two hours. Degraded service, meaning something functions poorly or a person is blocked: response the next business day. All other requests: acknowledged within one business day and scheduled.

Availability. Standard hours are Monday through Friday, with Saturday scheduling by agreement. Wednesday after 6:00 PM and Sundays are generally unavailable except where separately agreed.

Single point of contact. Chris designates one person authorized to approve work; Keenan is the corresponding contact. Requests received through other channels are logged and referred to the designated contact rather than performed.

5. Changes, purchases and approvals

Scope changes. If a materially significant matter arises that was not contemplated at the outset and would substantially alter the time, resources or responsibilities required, the parties review it together before work begins. Any adjustment to scope or pricing is agreed in writing in advance. No additional professional-service charges are incurred without prior discussion and approval.

Purchases. Hardware, licenses, subscriptions and third-party services are purchased in Chris's name and on her accounts. Any purchase over $500, and any new recurring subscription regardless of amount, requires her prior approval. Equipment and vendor invoices are not marked up. Any referral or partner compensation available to either firm on a recommended product is disclosed before purchase.

6. Initial ninety days

Days 1 to 30. Full inventory of systems, accounts, cameras, vendors, contracts and renewal dates. All accounts and credentials placed in Chris's name within a password manager she owns. Written separation plan from the former parent organization. Initial assessment of recurring cost and operational risk.

Days 31 to 60. Migration underway in risk-reducing order. Duplicate and unused subscriptions cancelled, with savings identified. Draft business-unit structure. Documentation and handoff of the two or three processes consuming the most owner time.

Days 61 to 90. Office, surveillance and dispatch technology progressing on a dated plan. Web and client-facing systems live or scheduled. Written roadmap for the following two quarters with named owners and dates. End-of-period review.

Review criteria. Whether the separation is complete or dated; whether recurring technology spend is lower than at commencement or explained; whether the number of matters requiring Chris personally has decreased; and whether the next two quarters have a plan with named owners.

7. Ownership of accounts, data and work product

  • Accounts and credentials. Accounts are created in Chris's name and under her domain, with Chris holding the ownership role on each platform. No account is registered under a vendor's ownership.
  • Data. Footage, logs, client records, documents and other materials produced for or by the business remain Chris's property during and after the engagement.
  • Work product. Configurations, documentation, runbooks, site plans and websites produced for Chris are hers to retain and to transfer to any other provider.
  • Pre-existing materials. General methods, templates and internal tooling brought to the engagement remain the property of the respective firm, with Chris retaining a license to any such materials installed or deployed for her.
  • Transition. On termination by either party, up to 30 days of transition assistance is provided at no additional monthly fee, including documentation, credential handover and a briefing for any successor provider.

8. Inclusions, exclusions and conduct

Included. For the Ownership and Transformation tiers, professional labor associated with the agreed initiatives is included in the monthly partnership fee.

Excluded. Unless expressly stated, monthly fees do not include hardware, equipment, software licenses or subscriptions, hosting, domains, construction, cabling, third-party contractors or vendor charges, materials, significant travel, or other external project expenses.

Subcontractors. Either firm may engage specialists for defined work, with prior notice to Chris. Keenan remains responsible for the resulting work and for any person he engages, and all such persons are bound by the same confidentiality obligations.

Confidentiality. Information learned about Chris's business, her clients and her sites is held in confidence during and after the engagement. The same obligation applies to information the parties learn about each other's methods. Neither party solicits the other's clients or personnel during the engagement or for twelve months following, except by written agreement.

Independence and insurance. Keenan Miller Technology Solutions and Joe CXO are independent businesses and not employees, and each maintains its own insurance. Neither firm holds an exclusive claim on Chris's security clients or other vendors.

9. Levels of responsibility and fees

$2,000 / monthTier 1 — Support (IT Essentials). "Keep my technology working." Twelve service hours per month, on site or remote; unused hours roll forward to a maximum bank of 24, with no cash value, expiring at the end of the engagement; additional support at $125 per hour; day-to-day IT, devices, Microsoft 365 and basic networking. Major initiatives and buildouts are outside this tier.
$5,500 / monthTier 2 — Ownership (Technology Partner). "Take ownership of my technology." Everything in Support, plus priority weekday scheduling; separation and migration from the former parent organization; Microsoft 365, document and collaboration systems; office technology planning and buildout; surveillance-center and dispatch-center technology; technology consulting for security-contracted clients; website creation, migration and maintenance; vendor coordination for new initiatives.
$10,000 / monthTier 3 — Transformation (Technology + Business Partner). Recommended. "Help me build what comes next." Everything in Ownership, plus Joe CXO: operational and process review; cost-efficiency analysis; financial and process cleanup; business-unit structure; planning and buildout of newer parts of the business; roadmap alignment; evaluation of vendors and partnerships; recurring executive strategy. Joseph's work is objective and milestone driven and is performed on the business rather than as day-to-day staff within it.

Tier 3 is recommended because the current transition involves both technology execution and decisions about how the businesses themselves should be structured and operated.

10. Commercial terms

  • Payment monthly in full on the 1st, or semi-monthly at 50% on the 1st and 50% on the 15th, at Chris's election.
  • An earlier commencement during September is available, prorated to the agreed start date.
  • Where an invoice remains unpaid, work is paused rather than accrued. No fee is charged for a period in which work was not performed.
  • External costs are billed at cost with supporting invoices, or purchased directly on Chris's accounts.
  • The initial arrangement may include a 30-day written termination option, subject to the final service agreement.
