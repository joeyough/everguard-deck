Technology Partnership Proposal

Prepared for Chris Calderon
Initial partnership period: October 1 to December 31, 2026. An earlier start in September is available, prorated, by mutual agreement.

The situation

The work in front of you is bigger than day-to-day IT. You are running current operations, separating technology from the former parent organization, standing up new offices and centers, building the digital side, and supporting technology for your own security clients. All at once.

This proposal is built around that, not around a block of hours. Each tier below is a level of responsibility and access, not a bigger bucket of labor. The ladder is simple: keep my technology working, take ownership of my technology, help me build the business.

Two seats, one team

KEENAN MILLER
Technology · Execution · Local presence

Keenan builds and operates the systems. On the island. Stack, cameras, access, migrations, the days that need a body on site.

JOE CXO
Executive · Operational · Financial strategy

Joseph is the CXO. You work in the business. He works on it. What gets fixed first, so the same problem does not get talked about four times.

One team. Not two vendors. Not a menu of hours.

What each tier covers

01   Support

$2,000 / month
Dependable local help for the everyday.
  • 12 included service hours a month for on-site or remote IT support.
  • Unused hours roll forward while the engagement is active, to a maximum bank of 24. Banked hours have no cash value and expire when the engagement ends.
  • Additional standard support billed at $125 an hour.
  • Typical work: device and workstation setup, software troubleshooting, Microsoft 365, basic networking, vendor coordination, routine maintenance.
  • Major initiatives and business buildouts sit outside this tier. They are scoped separately or handled under Ownership.

02   Ownership

$5,500 / month
From IT support to IT ownership.

Keenan becomes your primary local technology resource, with priority weekday scheduling for operations, active projects and agreed initiatives. The tier is built around responsibility and availability rather than an hourly allowance.

  • Day-to-day local and remote IT support.
  • Technology separation and migration from the former parent organization.
  • Microsoft 365, document and collaboration systems.
  • Office technology planning and buildout.
  • Surveillance-center and dispatch-center technology, planned, coordinated and implemented.
  • Technology consulting and project help for your security-contracted clients.
  • Website creation, migration and maintenance for agreed properties.
  • Vendor coordination and execution for new business initiatives.
Priority availability

Ownership and Transformation receive priority scheduling for weekday needs and active projects. Standard availability is Monday to Friday, with Saturday scheduling by agreement. Wednesday after 6:00 PM and Sundays are generally unavailable unless separately agreed.

03   Transformation

$10,000 / month
One partnership. Two complementary perspectives.

Everything in Ownership, plus Joe CXO. Keenan executes the technology. Joseph strengthens the business around it. His work is objective and milestone driven: he works on the business rather than functioning as day-to-day staff inside it.

  • Operational and process review, aimed at making the business easier to run and to scale.
  • Cost-efficiency analysis and removal of unnecessary operating expense.
  • Financial and process cleanup, with better visibility and better decisions around the numbers.
  • Business-unit structure: which activities, systems, contracts and operations belong under which business as you move off the former parent company.
  • Planning and functional buildout of the newer parts of the business.
  • Roadmap alignment, so the systems support the operating model instead of dictating it.
  • Evaluation of vendors, strategic relationships and potential partnerships.
  • Recurring executive strategy on priorities, risks, growth and the next stage of the organization.

Clear boundaries

Ownership and Transformation are meant to be complete professional-service engagements for the technology needs and projects identified, and reasonably expected, at the start of the initial period.

Included

For Tiers 2 and 3, Keenan's professional labor on the agreed initiatives is included in the monthly fee. The point is a partnership, not a stream of surprise invoices.

Not included

Unless stated otherwise, monthly fees exclude hardware, equipment, software licenses and subscriptions, hosting, domains, construction, cabling, third-party contractors and vendor charges, materials, significant travel, and other external project costs.

Materially new or unusually large projects

If something significant comes up that was not contemplated at the outset and would substantially change the time, resources or responsibility involved, both sides review it together before work begins. Any change to scope or price is agreed in writing first. No additional professional-service charges are incurred without prior discussion and approval.

The first 90 days

October 1 to December 31, 2026.

The initial period exists to prove the value and find the real operating rhythm of the relationship. At the end of it, you and Keenan review workload, utilization, value delivered, active and upcoming projects, responsibilities, and the service level that makes sense going forward.

After that review the partnership may continue month to month, renew for another 90 days, or move to a longer-term agreement such as an annual partnership. The initial arrangement may include a 30-day written termination option, subject to the final service agreement.
